How It Works

Pick a Subject

Choose a department and topic, or describe what you want to learn. The system generates a structured course with weekly topics and learning objectives.

Meet Your Professor

Each course gets a professor with a unique personality, teaching style, accent, and voice. You can customize them or let the system surprise you.

Listen & Share

AI generates full lecture content in the professor's voice and converts it to audio. Listen in the browser, download, or share with the community.

Design note — How It Works: Three steps max. This addresses the "wait, how does this actually work?" question that the customer persona has after seeing the CTA. Keep it concise — this isn't a features page, it's reassurance.
