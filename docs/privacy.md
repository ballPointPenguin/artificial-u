# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: March 28, 2026**

## The Short Version

We collect very little data about you. We don't sell it, we don't track you across the web, we don't show you ads. Most of this site works without an account. If you do create one, we store your email and username so you can log back in.

That's it. That's the privacy policy.

## What We Collect (and Why)

### If You Browse Without Logging In

**Almost nothing.** You can view courses, listen to lectures, and download audio files without an account.

We use [Plausible Analytics](https://plausible.io/)—a privacy-focused analytics service that collects:

- Page views (which pages get visited)
- General geographic region (country/city level, not precise location)
- Browser and device type
- Referral sources (how you found us)

Plausible doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you across sites, and doesn't collect personally identifiable information. We use it to understand which content is popular and where our visitors come from. That's it.

### If You Create an Account

**Authentication:** We use [Auth0](https://auth0.com/) for secure authentication. When you sign up, we require:

- **Email address** (for login and account recovery)
- **Username** (which you can set or change at any time)

Auth0 may set cookies in your browser to keep you logged in. We don't control these cookies—they're part of Auth0's authentication system.

**Profile data:** We store your email and username on our servers so you can create courses, lectures, and professors. That's all we keep.

**Preferences:** Some display preferences (theme choice, volume settings, etc.) may be saved in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device.

### What We Don't Collect

- **No browsing history:** We don't track what you view, listen to, or download
- **No behavioral tracking:** We don't build profiles of your interests or habits
- **No third-party tracking:** No Facebook Pixel, no Google Analytics, no ad networks
- **No precise location:** We don't collect GPS data or precise geolocation
- **No personal data beyond email/username:** We don't ask for your real name, address, phone number, or anything else

## How We Use Your Data

- **Email:** To let you log in and (if necessary) reset your password. We won't email you marketing unless you explicitly opt in.
- **Username:** To attribute content you create (courses, lectures, professors) to your account.
- **Analytics:** To understand traffic patterns and improve the site.

That's the complete list.

## Who We Share Data With

**Almost nobody.**

- **Auth0:** They handle authentication, so they see your email when you log in.
- **Plausible Analytics:** They see anonymous page views—no personal data.
- **No one else:** We don't sell data. We don't share it with advertisers, data brokers, or anyone else.

### Legal Requests

We'll comply with valid legal requests (court orders, subpoenas) if required by law. We'll notify you unless prohibited from doing so.

## Where Your Data Lives

Our servers are hosted on **Amazon Web Services (AWS)** in the United States. If you're in the EU, UK, or another region with strong data protection laws, this means your data crosses international borders.

We don't currently participate in Privacy Shield or similar frameworks because we collect so little data it's not relevant. But if you're concerned about this, you're welcome to use the site without creating an account.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access your data:** Email us and we'll tell you what we have (spoiler: email and username).
- **Correct your data:** You can change your username in account settings. Email us if you need to update your email.
- **Delete your data:** Email us requesting account deletion and we'll permanently remove your account, email, and username within 30 days. Note: any content you've published (courses, lectures) will remain under Creative Commons license, but we'll disassociate it from your account.
- **Export your data:** We'll provide a JSON file of your account data if you request it.

## Data Retention

- **Active accounts:** We keep your email and username as long as your account exists.
- **Deleted accounts:** Permanently removed within 30 days of your deletion request.
- **Analytics:** Plausible retains anonymous data for 30 days, then aggregates it.

## Security

We use industry-standard security practices:

- Encrypted connections (HTTPS)
- Secure authentication via Auth0
- Regular security updates
- No storage of passwords (Auth0 handles that)

But let's be honest: no system is perfectly secure. We do our best, but we can't guarantee absolute security.

## Children's Privacy

ArtificialU is not directed at children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we'll delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy occasionally. We'll note the revision date at the top. Material changes will be announced on the site.

## No Ads, No Tracking, No BS

This is a passion project, not a surveillance platform. We're not here to monetize your attention or sell your data. We just want to explore what AI can do with educational content.

If you have questions or concerns about privacy, email us at: <ben@aliencyb.org>

**In summary:** Use the site without an account if you want maximum privacy. Create an account if you want to make stuff. Either way, we're not tracking you.
